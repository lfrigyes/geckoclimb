Gecko Climb – Delete your account

An account is optional in Gecko Climb; it only keeps a copy of your climbing data in the cloud so it syncs between your phones.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Gecko Climb and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

This deletes at once your account (email address and password) and the cloud copy of your climbing data. The climbs stored on your phone stay there until you delete the app.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to geckoclimb.app@gmail.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Nothing from your account is kept after deletion. Ad data held by Google follows Google’s policies.
