Personal WhatsApp assistant: privacy notice

Last updated: 27 October 2026

What this is

A small private assistant that its owner (Frigyes Levente) uses through WhatsApp: a short daily Arabic practice chat and a daily training check-in. It is not a public service. It talks to one person only, its owner, and ignores messages from every other number. It is separate from the Gecko Climb app.

What it processes

  • The owner's phone number and the text of the messages the owner sends to the assistant.
  • The owner's own study and training records, which the assistant reads from the owner's computer to write the daily messages.

Where it goes

  • The assistant runs on the owner's own computer. Message state and logs are stored there, not on a public server.
  • To write a reply, the text of a message and a short summary of the owner's records are sent to Anthropic's Claude service, which generates the reply.
  • WhatsApp messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, under Meta's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or given to anyone else.

How long it is kept

Message identifiers used to avoid duplicates are deleted after 7 days. Technical logs contain no message text. The owner can delete all stored assistant data at any time.

Contact and deletion

Questions or a deletion request: geckoclimb.app@gmail.com.
