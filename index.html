Gecko Climb

A colourful climbing tracker for boulder, top rope and lead. Log your gym's routes and every try, see how high you got, and watch Tex the gecko climb with you.

Coming soon to Google Play
🧗 Every try counts

Slide Tex up the wall to where you fell, or top it. Lead climbs count your clipped quickdraws.

📐 The real wall

Wall height, overhangs, roofs and slabs, even a steep section in the middle of a route.

📊 Your progress

Grade pyramid, tries until the send, where you fall, and the muscle load of your sessions.

🔒 Your data

Stays on your phone. An account is optional, only to sync between phones.
